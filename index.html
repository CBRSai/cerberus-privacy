Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy describes the current private CERBERUS prototype. The WHOOP connection is not yet active. This policy will be updated before that connection is enabled or the service is offered to other users.

About CERBERUS

CERBERUS is a personal dashboard project for organising tasks, daily focus and fitness information. Privacy enquiries can be sent to cbrsai.hq@gmail.com. CERBERUS is the project name.

Information used by the current prototype

  • Dashboard content: tasks, due dates and times, completion status and daily focus entered by the user are saved in the browser’s local storage.
  • Local account: the app stores a username, a salted password hash, hashed session tokens, session expiry times and failed sign-in timestamps on the Mac running the app. It does not store the account password in plain text.
  • Session cookie: an essential cookie keeps the user signed in. Sessions expire after eight hours, or can be ended by signing out.
  • Support correspondence: if you email the project, your address and the information you send are processed through the project’s Gmail mailbox.

This information is used to provide dashboard features, control access, limit repeated failed sign-in attempts and respond to enquiries. Local browser storage is not encrypted by the app. Device security and access to the Mac remain important.

Planned WHOOP connection

The current prototype does not retrieve WHOOP data. A future connection is intended to request your permission through WHOOP’s authorisation screen to read recovery, sleep, physiological cycles and workouts, and to maintain the connection using refresh credentials. These permissions are intended to display fitness information within CERBERUS.

Before enabling this feature, this policy will explain the actual storage location, retention period, service providers, and how to disconnect and delete imported data. Connecting WHOOP will require a separate authorisation step.

Sharing and external services

The current dashboard does not send task or focus content to an AI service and has no advertising or data-selling feature. Email correspondence is handled by Google’s Gmail service. Development tools may produce operational logs and framework telemetry; this policy does not claim that the development environment has no external connections.

A publicly hosted copy of this policy may involve a hosting provider processing visitor connection information, such as IP addresses and access logs. The provider and applicable details will be identified before publication.

Storage and deletion

Dashboard content remains in the browser until removed through the app or by clearing the site’s browser data. Signing out does not delete it. Exported copies remain wherever the user saves them.

Local account records remain on the Mac until removed. Expired session records may remain until a later sign-in cleans them up. The prototype does not currently provide automated account deletion or password recovery. Contact the project for help with these actions. Email enquiries are retained as needed to respond and keep relevant correspondence; there is no automatic mailbox deletion schedule currently configured.

Your choices and privacy enquiries

You can edit or remove dashboard content, clear browser storage, sign out, and choose not to connect external services. To ask about information held by the project or request access, correction or deletion, email cbrsai.hq@gmail.com. Applicable privacy rights depend on the circumstances and jurisdiction.

Changes

This policy will be revised when data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.
